Deep Ocean

Deep Ocean is a refreshing aquatic fragrance that captures the essence of the sea. Crisp citrus notes blend with cool marine accords, while earthy vetiver and warm amber add depth and sophistication. Perfect for those who crave a clean and energizing scent that lasts all day.

AED 120.00

Fragrance Notes:

  • Top Notes: Zesty Lemon, Sea Spray, Green Mandarin
  • Heart Notes: Aquatic Accord, Driftwood, Blue Sage
  • Base Notes: Vetiver, Ambergris, White Musk

Longevity: Long lasting 8-10 hours

Best For: Daytime adventures, beach outings, warm-weather freshness
