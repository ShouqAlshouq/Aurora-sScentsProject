Midnight Storm

Midnight Storm is a bold and intense fragrance that exudes mystery and strength. The invigorating freshness of bergamot leads into a heart of spicy patchouli and deep, smoky oud, creating an unforgettable and masculine scent perfect for evening wear and special occasions.

AED 100.00

Fragrance Notes:

  • Top Notes: Bergamot, Black Pepper, Saffron
  • Heart Notes: Patchouli, Leather Accord, Dark Woods
  • Base Notes: Smoky Oud, Amber, Tonka Bean

Longevity: Intense and long-lasting (12+ hours)

Best For: Nighttime events, special occasions, bold statements
