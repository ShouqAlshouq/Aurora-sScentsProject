Ocean Breeze

Ocean Breeze captures the essence of the sea, blending invigorating citrus, fresh florals, and a touch of aquatic notes for a refreshing and clean scent. Ideal for day wear, it brings the spirit of the ocean to life, leaving a light and breezy trail that lasts throughout the day.

AED 120.00

Fragrance Notes:

  • Top Notes: Lemon, Grapefruit, Sea Breeze
  • Heart Notes: Jasmine, Lily of the Valley, Marine Accord
  • Base Notes: Musk, Cedarwood, Amber

Longevity: Normal (4-6 hours)

Best For: Daytime wear, casual outings, beach days
