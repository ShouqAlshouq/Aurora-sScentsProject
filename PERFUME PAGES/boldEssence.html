Bold Essence

Bold Essence is a sophisticated and invigorating fragrance that embodies confidence and strength. With fresh citrus opening notes, a heart of aromatic spices, and a deep woody base, this scent is perfect for the modern man who wants to leave a lasting impression.

AED 90.00

Fragrance Notes:

  • Top Notes: Bergamot, Lemon Zest, Marine Accord
  • Heart Notes: Black Pepper, Lavender, Geranium
  • Base Notes: Cedarwood, Ambergris, Vetiver

Longevity: Moderate (8-10 hours)

Best For: Business meetings, night outs, casual sophistication
