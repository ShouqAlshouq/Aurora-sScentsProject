Gentle Rose

Gentle Rose is a timeless floral fragrance that embraces the soft elegance of blooming roses. Blended with delicate peony, creamy sandalwood, and a hint of sweet vanilla, this scent creates a graceful and feminine aura perfect for any occasion.

AED 150.00

Fragrance Notes:

  • Top Notes: Rose Petals, Bergamot, Pink Pepper
  • Heart Notes: Peony, Jasmine, White Lily
  • Base Notes: Sandalwood, Vanilla, Amber

Longevity: Long-lasting (10-12 hours)

Best For: Special occasions, everyday elegance
